    Problems

    The curve you draw is a cubic between neighbouring joints, set by the position and slope at each joint, the same shape a beam element uses. Joints move only in ways that keep every member its original length, and supports hold them. The slopes are yours: a wall should keep its slope at zero, a hinge may kink, a rigid corner keeps its right angle. Check compares the signs of your curvature with the bending moment, segment by segment, and the direction each joint moves. Sizes are never graded. For beams the moment diagram is drawn on its own axis above the beam, positive up; for frames it is drawn on the compression side of each member, and the sign follows the member's own axis (beams left to right, columns bottom to top). The round button in the corner of the drawing flips it to the other side.
